# Grilling

Interview the user relentlessly down the plan's design tree to shared understanding.

## Workflow

1. Map the plan's design tree — decisions and dependencies.
2. Take one branch; ask its first unresolved decision.
3. Follow the answer to its dependent decisions; settle them before another branch.
4. Repeat until every important branch is resolved or its uncertainty explicit.
5. Close with a compact summary — confirmed facts, decisions made, assumptions, unresolved questions, recommended next step.

Done when every important branch is resolved (or its uncertainty explicit), you and the user share understanding, and the closing summary is delivered.

## Rules

- Ask one question at a time — never batch.
- Give every question your recommended answer.
- If available sources can answer it, investigate instead of asking.
- Grill the plan relentlessly, not the person.
